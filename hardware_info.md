# Hardware Specifications

## Controller
- **Model:** Raspberry Zero 2 W
- **Communication:** SSH
- **IP Address:** 192.160.0.38

## Audio Amplifier
- **Model:** MAX98357A

## Servo (Right and Left)
- **Model:** SCS0009
- **Communication:** UART, Half Duplex

## Accelerometer and Gyro Sensor
- **Model:** MPU-6050 IMU
- **Communication:** I2C

## Camera
- **Model:** OV5647
- **Configuration & Register Control:** I2C
- **Raw Video and Image Data:** MIPI CSI-2

## Microphone
- **Model:** INMP441
- **Communication:** I2C

## LED
- **Model:** WS2812
